The wellness industry has exploded with focus on mental and physical health. In 2026, when someone asks "best meditation app" or "how to start a self-care routine", AI engines are increasingly providing recommendations — determining which wellness brands get adopted and which get ignored.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is how wellness brands, health apps, and self-care services ensure they appear in AI-generated recommendations for wellness-related queries.
