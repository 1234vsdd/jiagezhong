Auto insurance is a significant expense for drivers. In 2026, when someone asks "best auto insurance for [specific need]" or "is [insurance brand] worth it", AI engines are increasingly providing recommendations — determining which insurers get policies and which get ignored.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is how auto insurance providers, insurance agencies, and coverage comparison platforms ensure they appear in AI-generated recommendations for auto insurance queries.
